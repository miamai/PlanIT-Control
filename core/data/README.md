# EIA reference-site snapshot

`eia860m_august_2026_sites.csv` contains 153 unique operating power plants
selected from the official August 2026 EIA-860M workbook published by the U.S.
Energy Information Administration on September 24, 2026.

Source workbook:
https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860m/xls/august_generator2026.xlsx

The snapshot contains up to three plants from each available U.S. state or
territory code, followed by additional plants in ascending EIA Plant ID order.
Generator rows were deduplicated by Plant ID. The technology and energy source
shown for each plant are those with the greatest summed operating nameplate
capacity in the workbook. Coordinates and names are not fabricated.

These plants are demonstration monitoring/reference locations. They are not
asserted to be active construction sites.
